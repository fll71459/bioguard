Stopping wildfire erosion before it buries streams, homes and habitat

After a wildfire, rain on bare slopes washes soil, ash and boulders into creeks and towns. Our solution scans burn scars with LiDAR drones, pinpoints the slopes most likely to erode, and sends a team of robots to mulch, lay wattles and seed native plants — then re-scans to prove it worked. Explore the seven parts of our project below, including what experts told us and how we improved.
